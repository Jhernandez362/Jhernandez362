## Featured Project

### TrainingTiles — VR Rhythm & Fitness Game

**Commercial VR title released on Steam**

TrainingTiles is a virtual reality game built around physical interaction,
rhythm-based gameplay and movement. It features five different minigames
designed to keep players active while competing for higher scores.

I worked on the project as a developer at **HumanBionics**, contributing
to the development of its immersive gameplay and VR interaction systems.

**Highlights**

- Published commercially on **Steam**
- Developed with **Unity**
- Virtual Reality development
- Physical and rhythm-based gameplay systems
- Interaction using VR controllers and hand-based mechanics
- Multiple gameplay modes with scoring and progression
- OpenXR-based VR support

**Role:** Developer  
**Company:** HumanBionics  
**Release:** November 2024
